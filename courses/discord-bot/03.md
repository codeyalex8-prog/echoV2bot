# Step 2: Create the bot in the Developer Portal
A bot is an **application** on Discord's side. Let's create one.

1. Open **discord.com/developers/applications** and log in.
2. Click **New Application**, name it (this is your bot's default name) and accept the terms.
3. On the left, open **Bot**. You can change its username and avatar here.
4. Click **Reset Token**, confirm, then **Copy**. Paste it somewhere safe for now.

**Warning: the token is your bot's password.** Anyone who has it controls your bot. Never post it in Discord, never put it on GitHub, never show it in a screenshot. If it leaks, press **Reset Token** straight away.

**Privileged intents (same Bot page)**
Scroll to **Privileged Gateway Intents**. Intents decide which events Discord sends your bot.
- **Server Members Intent**: turn ON (needed for welcome messages on page 12)
- **Message Content Intent**: leave OFF unless your bot must read normal chat messages (slash commands do not need it)
- **Presence Intent**: leave OFF

Press **Save Changes**.

**Collect two more IDs**
Turn on **Developer Mode** in Discord (User Settings > Advanced). Then:
- **Application ID**: Developer Portal > General Information > *Application ID* (this is your `CLIENT_ID`)
- **Server ID**: right-click your test server > **Copy Server ID** (this is your `GUILD_ID`)

**Checkpoint:** you have a token, an Application ID and a Server ID written down.
