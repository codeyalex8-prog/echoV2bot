# Step 3: Invite the bot to your server
Right now the bot exists but is in no server. Let's invite it.

1. In the Developer Portal open **OAuth2 > URL Generator**.
2. Under **Scopes** tick:
   - `bot`
   - `applications.commands` (required for slash commands)
3. Under **Bot Permissions** tick only what you need. A good starter set:
   - View Channels
   - Send Messages
   - Embed Links
   - Read Message History
   - Use Slash Commands
4. Copy the **Generated URL** at the bottom, open it in your browser, pick your test server and press **Authorize**.

The bot now appears in your member list, **offline** (it's not running yet, that's normal).

**Permissions: keep them small**
Giving **Administrator** is tempting, but if your token ever leaks the attacker gets full power. Only ask for what the bot really uses. You can always add more later by making a new invite link.

**Role order matters**
If your bot needs to manage roles or members, its role must sit **above** the roles it manages: Server Settings > Roles, then drag the bot's role higher.

**Checkpoint:** you can see your bot (offline) in the member list of your test server.
