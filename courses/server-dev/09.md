# Support and ticket systems
A ticket system keeps support **private, organised and trackable**.

**How it works**
1. A member presses a button on a **panel** (for example "Get Assistance")
2. The bot creates a **private channel** only they and staff can see
3. Staff help, then **close** the ticket
4. The bot saves a **transcript** to a staff log

**What a good ticket setup has**
- **Categories:** support, orders, reports, partnerships (different staff roles for each)
- **A form or first message:** ask for the key info up front (what, where, screenshots)
- **Claim button:** one staff member owns the ticket so two people don't answer at once
- **Close with reason + transcript log**
- **A cap per member** (one open ticket each) to stop spam
- **A blacklist** for abusers

**Response standards**
- Reply within a set time (say 24 hours) and say so on the panel
- Use short templates for common answers, but personalise them
- Escalate hard cases to senior staff instead of guessing

**Public support vs tickets**
- Use a **#help forum** for general questions everyone can learn from
- Use **tickets** for private things: payments, account details, reports

**Ticket etiquette to put on the panel**
- One issue per ticket
- Don't ping staff repeatedly
- Never share passwords or tokens

**Review your tickets monthly**
- How many opened and closed?
- Average time to first reply?
- What question comes up most? Add it to the **FAQ** channel and the number drops.

Checkpoint: open a test ticket yourself and walk the whole flow, from panel to transcript.
