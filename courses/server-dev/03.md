# Designing channels and categories
**Categories** group channels. Aim for **5 to 7 categories** at launch and **under 25 channels** total. A simple, proven layout:

```
INFORMATION
  welcome
  rules
  announcements
  faq

COMMUNITY
  general
  media
  suggestions
  off-topic

SUPPORT
  open-a-ticket
  help

SHOP / SERVICES   (if you sell something)
  services
  reviews
  portfolio

VOICE
  General Voice
  Study / Chill

STAFF  (hidden)
  staff-chat
  mod-logs
  staff-commands
```
**Naming**
- lowercase, short, clear: `bot-commands`, not `BOT COMMANDS!!!`
- Keep one style: all lowercase with dashes
- Don't mix long decorations; they break on mobile

**Channel topics:** every channel needs a one-line topic that says what belongs there.

**Read-only vs chat**
- Information channels: members can **view** but not send
- Announcements: use an **Announcement channel** so other servers can follow
- Chat channels: slow mode (5 to 10 seconds) in busy ones

**Threads and forums**
- **Forum channels** are perfect for support and suggestions: each post is its own thread with tags like *Open* or *Solved*
- Use **threads** for long discussions so the main channel stays readable

**Order matters:** put the most important channels at the top of each category, and put **rules** high in the list.

**Avoid:** a channel per tiny topic, channels nobody will use, and walls of emoji in names.

Checkpoint: draw your layout on paper first, then build it. Count categories and channels.
