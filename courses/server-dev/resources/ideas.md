# Ideas for your server
**Events**
- Weekly showcase where members share their work
- Monthly contest with a role as the prize
- "Ask the team anything" live Q&A
- Game or movie night in voice
- Challenge of the week with a leaderboard

**Channels that bring people back**
- `#introductions` with a prompt
- `#showcase` for members' projects
- `#suggestions` with vote reactions
- `#daily-question` with a new prompt each day
- A `#help` forum with tags like Open and Solved

**Roles and recognition**
- Member of the month
- Helper role for people who often assist others
- Booster and supporter roles with a small perk
- Level or activity roles

**Systems to add over time**
- Ticket system with transcripts
- Self-assign ping roles
- Giveaways and polls
- Welcome and verification flow
- Custom bot for your exact workflow

**Growth**
- Partner with similar-size servers
- Unique invite links per source to see what works
- Post your best content where your audience already is

**Keep in mind:** add one new thing at a time, see if people use it, and drop what doesn't work.
