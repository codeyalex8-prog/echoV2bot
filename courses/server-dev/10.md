# Growth and engagement
Members stay for **people and activity**, not for the number of channels.

**Content rhythm**
- **Daily:** a prompt or question in #general
- **Weekly:** an event (game night, Q&A, showcase, giveaway)
- **Monthly:** an announcement of what's new and what's next

**Event ideas that work**
- Showcase nights (members share their work)
- Game or build contests with a small prize
- "Ask the team anything" sessions
- Challenges with a role reward
- Movie or music nights in voice

**Announcements**
Use an **Announcement channel** and ping only the opt-in `Announcements` role. Keep posts short, with one clear call to action.

**Invites and discovery**
- Use **unique invite links** per source (a video, a partner) so you know what works
- Build a **vanity URL** when you unlock it
- If you qualify, the **Server Discovery** and Community features help (they require rules, activity and a healthy environment)
- **Partner carefully:** only with servers of a similar size and quality, and set the terms in writing

**Boosts and perks:** be transparent about what boosting gives, and thank boosters with a role.

**Retention beats reach**
A server that keeps 40% of new members beats one that gains 1,000 and loses 900. Greet people, answer fast and invite them to events.

**Recognise people**
Spotlight active members, give helpful ones a **Helper** role, and celebrate milestones.

**Use Server Insights**
Settings > Server Insights shows joins, retention, active members and busiest times. Schedule events when your members are online.

**Never buy members.** Fake members don't talk, hurt your stats and can get a server flagged.

Checkpoint: plan one event for next week and write the announcement.
