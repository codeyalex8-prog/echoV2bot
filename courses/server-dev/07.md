# Moderation that scales
Good moderation is **clear rules, consistent action and a record**.

**Layers of protection**
1. **Discord AutoMod:** block spam, mention spam, and chosen words; alert staff
2. **Verification level + account age:** keep out throwaway accounts
3. **A moderation bot:** warns, timeouts, bans, logs
4. **Humans:** mods who read context

**A punishment ladder** (write it down and stick to it)
- 1st offence: warning
- 2nd: timeout (10 min to 1 hour)
- 3rd: longer timeout or kick
- Severe (scams, threats, NSFW, raids): immediate ban

**Always log**
Create a private `#mod-logs` channel. Log: bans, kicks, timeouts, message deletes, role changes, joins. Staff should **write a reason** for every action.

**Appeals:** give banned members one place to appeal (a form or a ticket in a separate server). Review appeals as a team, not alone.

**Raid protection**
- Limit invites and set verification to High during an attack
- Turn on mention-spam AutoMod
- Have a "panic" checklist: lock channels, pause invites, enable slow mode

**Mod behaviour**
- Stay calm, never argue publicly: take it to DMs or a ticket
- Be consistent: same rule, same result for everyone, including friends
- Explain the rule broken, not "because I said so"
- Two mods for big decisions (permanent bans)

**Scams to watch for:** free Nitro links, "check my profile", fake support DMs, crypto "investments". Warn members regularly that staff never DM first asking for passwords or payments.

**Mod tools:** right click a member to **Timeout**; use **Audit Log** to check history; **Safety Setup** shows DM and spam protection.

Checkpoint: write your ladder, set up `#mod-logs`, and enable at least spam and mention AutoMod rules.
